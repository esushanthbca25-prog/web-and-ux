🌆 Explore Bengaluru South

Discover the best places to visit, eat, and enjoy!

🌳 Lalbagh Botanical Garden

A historic 240-acre garden with exotic plants and a stunning glasshouse. Perfect for nature lovers and photographers.

🍽️ Vidyarthi Bhavan

Iconic South Indian restaurant famous for crispy masala dosas and vintage charm. A must-visit for foodies!

🎉 Loco Bear Entertainment Hub

Indoor amusement park with bowling, laser tag, trampolines, and arcade games. Fun for all ages!
